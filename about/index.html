Who we are

Sahuarita Pro Plumbing is a local plumbing company serving Sahuarita and Green Valley from a base in Sahuarita. We handle water heaters, drains, sewer lines, leaks, fixtures, and commercial plumbing, and we take emergency calls at any hour.

This site does not list staff names, years in business, or review counts, because we only publish what we can stand behind. What we can say is how we work and what you can expect when you call.

How we work

When you call, you reach dispatch. We ask what you see, where the main valve is, and how to reach you, then send a plumber the same day when the schedule allows. Before any repair begins, you get a clear price.

We explain what failed and what the options are, including when a smaller repair makes more sense than a replacement. If a job is better planned than rushed, we say that too.

Licensing and how to check it

Arizona licenses plumbing contractors through the Registrar of Contractors. You can look up any contractor on the Registrar of Contractors website before you hire, and you should do that for every company you consider, including us. Ask us for our license details when you call.

Plumbing work is also governed by the plumbing code your local building department has adopted. Water heater connections follow published standards such as NFPA 54 for gas and NFPA 70 for electrical. We follow the manufacturer's instructions on top of those.

What we handle

Our work covers the plumbing problems that homes and small businesses in this area call about most. That includes water heaters, from repair to replacement and tankless units, plus drains and sewer lines, hidden leaks, fixtures, and commercial plumbing.

Emergencies such as burst pipes, slab leaks, and sewer backups get priority on our 24/7 line. Planned work, such as repiping or a remodel, is scheduled around your week.

What to expect from a visit

The plumber starts by asking what you have noticed, then looks at the fixture, the pipe, or the equipment involved. Before any repair begins, you get a clear price and an explanation of the options. If a smaller fix will do, we say so.

When the work is done, we explain what failed and what to watch for. For larger jobs, we tell you what to ask your insurer and which parts of the cleanup belong to other trades.

What this site leaves out on purpose

You will not find invented reviews, made-up customer counts, awards, or staff photos on these pages. Those claims are easy to fake and hard to check, and we would rather earn trust on the phone and at your door. Everything stated here is something we can stand behind.

Photos of real work will be added as they become available. Until then, the pages stay plain and factual.

How we think about pricing

Plumbing prices depend on the problem, the access, the parts, and the time of day, so a single list price would mislead you. We diagnose first and quote second. You decide after you see the number, and nothing starts before you agree.

If a repair and a replacement are close in cost, we explain the trade-offs, including how long each is likely to last. That conversation is part of the job, not an extra.

Safety comes before speed

Some problems cannot wait for a plumber. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 and your gas utility from outside. If water is rising near outlets, stay out of it and shut off the main valve only if you can reach it dry.

Once everyone is safe, call our line. We stay on the phone long enough to tell you what to do while a plumber is on the way.

Working with landlords, managers, and insurers

Property managers and landlords often need a written note of what we found and what we did. We provide one on request, so you have a record for owners and insurers. For commercial buildings, we plan work around your hours when we can.

After water damage, we repair the plumbing and tell you what to ask a restoration company to handle. We do not take on drying and demolition ourselves.

The places we know

Sahuarita is an incorporated town in Pima County, and Green Valley is an unincorporated community just south of it. Most homes in the area sit on concrete slabs, summers are long and hot, and winter brings occasional frost. Those facts shape our work, from where leaks hide to which pipes need protection.
