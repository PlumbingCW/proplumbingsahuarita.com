Do these four things now

Close the main water valve, usually at the meter box outside or where the line enters the house. Turn the handle clockwise until it stops. Then open a low faucet to drain the lines.

Switch off power to flooded rooms at the breaker if you can reach it dry. Never step into standing water near outlets. Move valuables, take photos, and call us.

What causes a pipe to burst in southern Arizona

Freeze damage is real in the Sahuarita area. Green Valley's records average around 37 frost nights per winter, which is enough to crack exposed pipes, irrigation backflow assemblies, hose bibs, and pool equipment. A cold snap after months of warm weather catches many homeowners off guard.

Other causes are high water pressure, corrosion, sun-damaged exterior PVC, failed supply lines, and settling. We find the cause as part of the repair, because replacing a split without fixing the pressure only invites the next one.

Signs a pipe is about to fail

Not every burst starts with a flood.

  • ✓Bulging, kinked, or frosted pipes in an attic, garage, or exterior wall during a freeze.
  • ✓Water stains on drywall or ceilings, especially after a cold night.
  • ✓A sudden drop in water pressure at a single fixture or the whole house.
  • ✓Banging pipes or a pressure gauge reading above normal.
  • ✓Green or white crust on copper joints or fittings, which indicates a slow leak.

Repair paths for a burst pipe

A split in a short section is repaired by cutting out the damaged stretch and installing new pipe, using couplings or soldered fittings appropriate to the material. Copper, PEX, and CPVC need different methods.

If the pipe is old, corroded in several spots, or the burst was caused by pressure, we recommend replacing a longer run. A pattern of repeated failures is a signal to talk about repiping. A burst under a slab is a slab leak, where the diagnosis is different.

After the repair: drying, documenting, and claims

Once the water is off, remove standing water, open cabinet doors, and run fans. Wet drywall and insulation hold moisture, so take photos and keep receipts. Restoration is a separate trade, and we can tell you what to ask for.

Ask your insurer what applies. Many homeowner policies cover sudden burst damage, though not frozen-pipe damage in a vacant home or the pipe itself. A report from us on the cause helps your claim.

Standards worth knowing after a burst

Replacement pipe and fittings should carry the marking of the standard they meet, such as ASTM F876 for PEX tubing. Mixing parts from different systems is a common cause of repeat leaks.

Protection against freezing, such as insulation or relocating exposed lines, is a matter of the plumbing code and the manufacturer's guidelines, so ask your building department what applies to your home.
