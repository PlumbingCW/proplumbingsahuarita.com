Garbage Disposal Repair and Installation in Sahuarita, AZ

A garbage disposal that hums but will not spin, leaks under the sink, or leaves the basin full of standing water is a quick fix in most cases. We repair and replace disposals in Sahuarita homes, and we tell you honestly when a repair is not worth the cost.

Before you call, switch the disposal off at the wall and never put your hand inside the unit. If the sink is backed up with no sound at all, the problem may be the drain line, not the disposal.

Why a disposal stops working

A humming disposal that does not spin is almost always jammed. A hard object, a stringy food, or a buildup of waste has locked the grinding plate. Many jams clear with the hex key slot underneath the unit, but the cause deserves a look.

A disposal that leaks is a different problem. The leak may come from the sink flange, the dishwasher connection, or the body of the unit itself. A leak at the body means the housing is cracked or the seals have failed, and the unit needs replacing.

Signs of a failing disposal

These problems tend to get worse if ignored.

  • ✓A steady hum with no grinding, or a motor that trips the breaker.
  • ✓Water dripping under the sink after you run the disposal.
  • ✓Slow draining or standing water in the sink.
  • ✓A burning smell, or a smell that persists after cleaning.
  • ✓Loud grinding noises that do not change with what you feed it.

Replacing a disposal

Most disposals last several years with normal use. When one fails, we remove it, check the sink flange and the drain connections, and install a new unit sized to your home. Motor strength matters more than the brand name for heavy use.

If your kitchen has a dishwasher, the disposal usually connects to the dishwasher drain, so we check that connection and the knockout plug on the new unit. A missed plug can make the dishwasher back up, which is a common DIY mistake.

What to keep out of the disposal

Grease, oils, and fats cool in the drain and build a coating that narrows the pipe over time. Coffee grounds, eggshells, pasta, and rice swell or pack into sludge. Fibrous foods like celery, corn husks, and potato peels wrap around the grinding parts.

Run cold water while the disposal works and for a short while after, so waste moves down the line instead of settling. If your kitchen drain has backed up before, our hydro jetting page explains how we clear a line that has built up grease.

Safety listing for disposals

Waste disposers are tested to the UL 430 standard, and the manufacturer's instructions say how they must be wired and connected. A disposer that is wired or drained in a way the manual does not allow can void its listing.

Never put your hand in the unit. Switch it off at the wall before any work.

Garbage disposal questions from Sahuarita homeowners

Why does my disposal hum but not spin?

It is usually jammed. Turn it off, and we clear the jam and reset the unit.

Is it safe to clear a jam myself?

Only with the power off at the wall and never with your hand. Use the hex key slot underneath or call us.

Can a leaking disposal be repaired?

A leak at the flange or a connection can be repaired. A leak from the body means the unit needs replacing.

How long does a disposal last?

Usually several years. Heavy use and hard foods shorten that.

Why does my sink back up when the disposal runs?

The drain line beyond the disposal is probably restricted. See drain cleaning.
