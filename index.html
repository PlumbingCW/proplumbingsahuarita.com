What counts as a plumbing emergency

Water you cannot stop, sewage coming up through a drain, a gas smell, or a heater leaking at the base are all emergencies. Close the main water valve for any flood. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 and your gas utility before you call us.

For everything else on this list, call our 24/7 line and we send a plumber the same day when the schedule allows. A clear price comes before work begins.

Plumbing in a desert town built on slabs

What that means for your home

Most homes in the Sahuarita area sit on concrete slabs, so drain lines run through or beneath the floor and many supply lines run through the attic. A leak can hide for weeks, and the first clue is often a water bill or a warm patch on the tile.

Desert conditions add their own wear. Summer heat shortens the life of water heaters in garages, mineral content in the water leaves scale on fixtures and in tanks, and winter frost can crack exposed lines. We plan repairs with all three in mind.

Plumbing work in the area follows the plumbing code each local building department adopts. The Town of Sahuarita and Pima County, which covers Green Valley, decide which edition and amendments apply, and a good plumber confirms them before starting. Before any digging, call Arizona 811, which says to call at least two full working days ahead.
