Plumber in Madera Highlands, Sahuarita, AZ

Madera Highlands is a master-planned community on the east side of Interstate 19 in Sahuarita, with views toward the Santa Rita Mountains. This page covers what we could confirm about it and how to prepare for a plumbing call.

About Madera Highlands

Town of Sahuarita development reports list permits in Madera Highlands Villages 4, 4A, 14, and 17 in April 2026. That tells us the community is organized in numbered villages and is still being built.

A local real estate guide calls it the more established of Sahuarita's master plans and places it east of Interstate 19. Meritage Homes has marketed newer sections, including Arbor at Madera Highlands II.

Madera Canyon, a well-known birdwatching area, lies just southeast of town.

New and established homes side by side

Some villages have been lived in for years while others are new. A newly built home may still be inside its builder's warranty, and warranty terms can limit who may repair a covered item.

If a problem shows up early, read the warranty in your closing documents before you call anyone. If the warranty has ended or does not cover the problem, call to ask about plumbing service.

Services that fit single-family homes

Homeowners with a heater that has failed or needs a planned swap can read about water heater installation. A dripping tap is covered under faucet repair, and a toilet that runs is covered under toilet repair.

Who to ask about water and sewer service

Your water bill shows your provider, and that provider can answer questions about pressure and the meter. For sewer questions, the Town of Sahuarita Wastewater Department can be reached at 520-344-7100.

Ask which provider serves your address before you assume responsibility for any line.

Madera Highlands plumbing questions

Where is Madera Highlands?

East of Interstate 19 in Sahuarita, ZIP 85629, according to a local guide and Town permit records. We do not state exact boundaries because the sources we found did not publish them.

Is Madera Highlands the same as Rancho Sahuarita?

No. They are separate master-planned communities with their own village names and permit records. Our Rancho Sahuarita page covers the other community.

My home is new. Who handles a leak?

Check your builder warranty first. If it has ended or does not cover the problem, call to ask about plumbing service. See leak detection.

How do I find my village number?

Your closing paperwork or the Pima County Assessor record lists the subdivision. A street address is enough for most calls.

What if I smell gas?

Leave the building and call 911 and your gas utility. Do not use switches or phones inside.
