Quail Creek is a gated community for residents aged 55 and older. It uses Green Valley mailing addresses, yet it also appears in the Town of Sahuarita's development reports. This page explains that overlap and how to prepare for a plumbing call.

About Quail Creek

The community's developer, Robson Resort Communities, describes gated entry, a 27-hole golf course, several clubhouses, and pickleball courts. Housing is intended for households with at least one resident aged 55 or older, with limited exceptions.

Its address in the developer's overview is 1100 N Eagle Hollow Road, Green Valley, ZIP 85614. The Town of Sahuarita's April 2026 development report lists building permits in Quail Creek II units and Quail Creek Block 1, plus a proposed 624-acre Quail Creek 2 Specific Plan still under review.

We could not confirm exactly where the Town boundary falls inside the community. For permits, ask the Town of Sahuarita and Pima County which one covers your address.

Gate access and community rules

Gated entry means a plumber cannot simply drive in. Ask your community office how to add a service visitor to the gate list, and give the plumber the process when you call.

The developer's overview mentions a homeowners association with its own website. Check its rules on contractor hours, vehicles, and exterior work before you schedule anything.

Leaving the house empty for weeks?

A leak in an empty home can run for days before anyone sees it. These steps are general advice, so follow your own association's guidance too.

  • ✓Ask a plumber whether you should close the main water shutoff before you leave.
  • ✓Ask a neighbor or property manager to look in on the house, with the gate arranged in advance.
  • ✓Turn down the water heater setting or switch it off, following the manufacturer's instructions.
  • ✓Check under sinks and around the heater for dampness before you leave and when you return.
  • ✓Keep the gate procedure and our number where a visitor can find them.

Quail Creek plumbing questions

Is Quail Creek in Sahuarita or Green Valley?

Both names appear. The mailing address is Green Valley, and the Town of Sahuarita lists permits in Quail Creek units. Ask the Town or Pima County about your address.

Who sets the rules for contractors in the community?

Your homeowners association does. Check its rules on gate access, hours, and exterior work before booking.

Should I shut off the water when I leave for the season?

Many people do, but the right choice depends on the home. Ask a plumber, and follow your association's guidance.

How do I get a plumber through the gate?

Ask the community office how to add a service visitor, and tell the plumber when you call.

Who do I ask about my water provider?

Your water bill names it. Ask them about pressure and the meter.

What if the water heater leaks while I am away?

Have someone close the main shutoff and call. See water heater replacement.
