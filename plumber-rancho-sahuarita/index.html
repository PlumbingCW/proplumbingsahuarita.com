Rancho Sahuarita is the large master-planned community on the north side of Sahuarita, built around a 10-acre lake with trails and parks. This page is for homeowners there who need plumbing help and want it explained plainly.

About Rancho Sahuarita

The community's developer says it opened in 2001 and now has more than 7,000 homes and 25,000 residents. Those are the developer's own figures, so treat them as approximate.

The developer also describes more than 30 miles of trails and more than 15 parks around the lake. Town of Sahuarita permit records place the Marketplace at Rancho Sahuarita at 15920 S Rancho Sahuarita Blvd.

The newest neighborhood is Entrada Del Toro. A local real estate guide places it between Sahuarita Road and El Toro Road, just off Interstate 19.

Building is still underway, so a Rancho Sahuarita address can mean a home from the early 2000s or one finished this year. Town records show new permits in Entrada Del Toro and other sections as recently as April 2026.

We do not guess what is inside your walls from the neighborhood name. The year built, the date on your water heater label, and your repair history tell you far more. The Pima County Assessor record for your address lists the year built.

Plumbing services homeowners here ask about

A typical single-family home needs help most often with a failed water heater, a slow or backed-up drain, or a fixture that leaks. For a clog that keeps coming back, see drain cleaning. For a damp spot or an unexplained high water bill, see leak detection.

Water and sewer: who to ask

Water and sewer service around Sahuarita is not handled by one provider for every home. Your bill names your water provider, and the Town runs a Wastewater Department that answers questions at 520-344-7100.

Before you pay for work on a main line, confirm which provider is responsible for the line in front of your house. That one call can decide who pays.

Rancho Sahuarita plumbing questions

Is Rancho Sahuarita part of the Town of Sahuarita?

Yes. The community uses Sahuarita addresses with ZIP 85629, and the Town's development reports list permits issued there.

How old are the homes in Rancho Sahuarita?

It depends on the section. The developer says the community opened in 2001, and permits were still being issued in April 2026. Check the year built for your own address.

Who do I ask about my water service?

Your water bill names the provider. Ask them about pressure, outages, and the meter.

Does my community association need to approve plumbing work?

Check your association's rules. Requirements for exterior work and contractor access vary.

How do I describe my location when I call?

Give your street, the nearest cross street, and the section name if you know it, such as Entrada Del Toro. The community is large and many streets look alike.

What should I do first if water is flowing?

Close the main shutoff, then call. For a pipe that has split, see burst pipe repair.
