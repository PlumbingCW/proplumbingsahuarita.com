How to choose the right page

If water is moving where it should not, start with burst pipe repair or leak detection. If the house has no hot water, start with water heater replacement or repair. If a drain is slow or backing up, start with drain cleaning.

Not sure? Call (866) 310-6010 and describe what you see. We point you to the right fix, even when it is a small one. For coverage details, see the Green Valley page or the coverage section on the home page.

Local conditions that shape plumbing here

Four things come up again and again in Sahuarita and Green Valley. Most homes are built on slabs, so drain lines and some supply lines are hidden. Summer heat is hard on water heaters in garages and attics. Mineral content in the water leaves scale on fixtures and in tanks. And a few frost nights each winter are enough to crack exposed pipes.

Each service page explains how those conditions affect that particular problem, so you can judge your own situation before you call. Where a published standard applies, such as NFPA 54 for gas piping or the EPA WaterSense specifications, the page names it.

How to describe a problem on the phone

You do not need plumbing words. Tell us what you see, what you hear, and when it started. For example, a meter that keeps moving, a warm patch on the floor, a toilet that gurgles when the washer runs, or a heater that is cold in the morning.

Also tell us where the main water valve is, if you know, and whether anyone has tried a fix. That helps us bring the right parts and gives you steps to follow while you wait.

Emergency work compared with planned work

Emergency calls are about stopping damage first, then fixing the cause. We shut off the water, clear the line, or secure the pipe, and then explain the repair. Planned work is the reverse, with more time to compare options and choose materials.

If you have a recurring problem, such as a drain that slows every few weeks, treat it as planned work before it becomes an emergency. A camera look or a pressure test now often costs less than a flooded floor later.
