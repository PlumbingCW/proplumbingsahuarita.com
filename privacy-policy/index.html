Last updated 2026-11-12.

What this website collects

This website has no forms, accounts, or comment fields, so it does not collect information you type in. The easiest way to reach us is by phone.

Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and reliability.

Phone calls

When you call (888) 910-8644, we may collect your phone number and what you tell us so we can dispatch a plumber and follow up on the job. Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

We use call information to schedule and complete your service. We do not sell it.

Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and some pages embed Google Maps. Those services may receive your IP address and browser details when the content loads, under Google's own privacy policy.

Cookies

We do not set advertising cookies. Third-party content such as an embedded map may set its own.

Your choices and contact

To ask about information we hold from a service call, call (888) 910-8644 or email contact@proplumbingsahuarita.com. We may need to confirm who you are first.
