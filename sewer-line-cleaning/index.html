Main line cleaning versus drain cleaning

A drain cleaning visit clears a fixture or a branch. Sewer line cleaning clears the main, the large pipe that carries everything from the house to the town sewer or the septic tank.

The tools are larger, the access point is a cleanout, and the cause is often different, with roots, grease, wipes, and sometimes a damaged pipe. If you are not sure which you need, our drain cleaning page explains the difference.

Emergency steps while you wait

Stop using water, including washers and dishwashers. Do not flush toilets. If wastewater is coming up in a tub or a floor drain, keep people and pets out and avoid contact without gloves.

Locate the cleanout cap near the foundation, but leave it closed unless a plumber tells you otherwise. Opening it can release a surge of wastewater.

Signs the main is the problem

Multiple fixtures acting up together is the giveaway.

  • ✓The lowest tub or shower backs up when the toilet flushes.
  • ✓Several drains gurgle after the washing machine drains.
  • ✓Floor drains overflow or a sewage smell rises from the yard cleanout.
  • ✓A single cleaning gave only short-term relief.
  • ✓The clog returned in weeks, not months.

Roots, wipes, and grease: the three usual causes

Roots enter through small gaps and grow into the flow. Desert trees such as mesquite push toward moisture, so even a modest crack can be enough. Flushable wipes do not break down the way paper does and gather in bends. Grease coats the pipe and shrinks it.

We clear what is blocking the line and then recommend a jet or a camera when the pattern suggests a return. See hydro jetting and sewer inspection for those steps.

When the Town may be involved

If your property is on town sewer and the blockage is in the public main, the Town of Sahuarita Wastewater Department handles it. A quick camera look shows whether the clog is on your side of the connection. Properties on septic systems involve different questions, since a full tank looks like a blocked line.

We tell you what we find. If the problem is beyond our work, we point you to the right party.

Sewer line cleaning questions in Sahuarita

Do you handle main sewer line emergencies at night or on weekends in Sahuarita?

Yes. Dispatch is 24/7, and we send a plumber the same day when the schedule allows.

How long does it take to clear a main line?

Many main line clears finish in one to two hours, longer when the line is long or heavily blocked.

Will the clog come back?

Not if we find the cause. Roots and grease can return, so we may recommend a jet or a camera.

Why do my floor drain and tub back up first?

They are the lowest points in the house, so wastewater exits there when the main is blocked.

Can I use drain cleaner on a main line clog?

No. It rarely works on a main, and it makes later work hazardous.
