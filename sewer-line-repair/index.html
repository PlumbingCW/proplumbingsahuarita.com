Sewer backup emergency: what to do first

Stop running water, including the washing machine and dishwasher. Flushing a toilet adds water that cannot leave the house. If wastewater is already coming up, keep children and pets out of the area and avoid touching it without gloves.

Do not use chemical drain cleaners in a backed-up main. They do not clear roots or collapsed pipe and make the later work hazardous. Call us and we send a plumber to confirm the cause.

Signs the sewer line is damaged

Backups that return after clearing point to damage.

  • ✓Several drains slow or gurgle at the same time.
  • ✓Toilets bubble when the washer or tub drains.
  • ✓The clog came back within weeks of the last cleaning.
  • ✓A wet or sunken patch or a persistent sewer smell in the yard.
  • ✓A camera look shows a crack, offset joint, or root mass.

How we find and fix the defect

We start with a camera inspection, which shows the pipe material, the defect, and the distance to it from the cleanout. A locator then marks the spot at the surface, so we know where to dig.

From there the choice is a spot repair, a section replacement, or a full lateral replacement. A short break may be a spot repair. A line with multiple cracks usually costs less over time as a full replacement. We explain the trade-offs before any work starts.

Pipe lining, cast iron, and what older homes may have

Pipe lining installs a resin liner inside the existing pipe. It suits cracks and joint leaks in a pipe that is still round and continuous, and it avoids a long trench. It is not a fit for a collapsed pipe or heavy offsets.

Where older cast iron exists, usually in homes that predate modern PVC and ABS, rust and scaling restrict flow and cracks leak. Cast iron repair may be a section swap to modern pipe. Check your home's age and tell us when you call.

Digging in Sahuarita: caliche, utilities, and Arizona 811

Caliche, the hard calcium carbonate layer common in southern Arizona soils, can slow trenching. We plan around it, which is another reason to choose a lining or a spot repair when the pipe allows.

Before any digging, Arizona 811 must be called so utilities can mark their lines. The service is free and it is the law. We handle the call as part of a repair, and if you are digging on your own, call 811 first.

Standards for lining and permits

Trenchless lining follows ASTM F1216, the standard practice for installing a resin-impregnated tube inside an existing pipe by inversion and curing. Ask any contractor which standard the proposed liner is installed to.

Under-slab repairs often need a permit and an inspection. Permit rules vary, so ask the Town of Sahuarita building safety department, or Pima County if you live in Green Valley.
