How a tankless heater works and what it needs

A tankless unit heats water as it flows, so there is no storage tank to run out. Gas units burn fuel through a heat exchanger and vent the exhaust, while electric units use heavy elements that draw large amounts of power.

That means the install depends on the house. Gas models need a gas line sized for the burner, a vent path that meets the manufacturer's rules, and a condensate drain on high-efficiency units. Electric models need panel capacity and a dedicated circuit, which is why they are rarely the first choice for a large family.

Signs a tankless heater needs service

Many of these have a quick fix.

  • ✓Hot water that goes cold and warm in short cycles, which can mean scale in the heat exchanger or a flow sensor issue.
  • ✓An error code on the display that returns after a reset.
  • ✓Reduced flow at fixtures even though the cold side is fine.
  • ✓Visible scale or a white crust at the inlet or on the isolation valves.
  • ✓A unit that has gone more than a year or two without a flush.

Descale and flush: the maintenance that matters most

Scale is the main enemy of tankless heaters. As water passes through the heat exchanger, minerals leave a film that narrows the passages and forces the unit to work harder. A descale flush circulates a mild acid solution through the heat exchanger, then rinses it clear.

The right schedule depends on your water. Because southern Arizona groundwater carries dissolved minerals, a yearly flush is a sensible default, and a softener or filter reduces the load. Your utility's annual water quality report shows how your supply compares.

Guidelines that govern a gas tankless install

A gas tankless unit is listed by a recognized testing laboratory, and the manufacturer's venting instructions are part of that listing. A vent material or run that the manual does not allow can void both the listing and the warranty.

Gas line sizing follows the tables in NFPA 54, the National Fuel Gas Code. That is why we size the line from the unit's input rating instead of guessing.

Tankless water heater questions in Sahuarita

How often should a tankless heater be flushed?

Many manufacturers recommend yearly, and local groundwater makes that a sensible default. We check the inlet screen and scale level during each visit.

Will a tankless heater work during a power outage?

Most need electricity for controls even on gas models. Ask us about backup options such as a battery unit or a small generator.

Can you repair tankless units from any brand?

We diagnose the common brands and many error codes. If a part must be ordered, we explain timing before we start.

Is a tankless heater worth it for a small household?

Often yes, especially when space matters. We compare your hot water habits with the heater capacity before you decide.

What about the gas line?

We check the gas line size, the meter, and the vent before quoting. Never relocate a gas appliance without a plumber and permit check from the Town of Sahuarita.
