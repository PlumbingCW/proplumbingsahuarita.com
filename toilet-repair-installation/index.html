Toilet Repair and Installation in Sahuarita, AZ

A toilet that runs all night, clogs twice a week, or leaks at the base wastes water and can damage the floor under it. We repair most toilets in a single visit and install new ones when the old one has reached the end of its life.

If the bowl is about to overflow right now, turn the water off at the valve behind the toilet and call. A backup that reaches several fixtures belongs on our drain cleaning page.

Why a toilet runs, clogs, or leaks

A toilet that keeps running usually has a worn flapper, a flush valve that no longer seals, or a fill valve that never shuts off. These parts wear out with age and with mineral buildup, and replacing them is routine work.

A toilet that clogs often is a different problem. Sometimes the cause is a partial blockage in the trap or the drain line beyond it. Sometimes it is an older low-flow model that struggles with modern use. We test which one you have before recommending a fix.

Signs your toilet needs attention

Most toilet trouble gives warning before it gets expensive.

  • ✓The tank refills by itself every few minutes, or you hear a faint hiss when nobody has flushed.
  • ✓Water seeps around the base, or the floor near it feels soft.
  • ✓The toilet rocks when you sit on it, which means the seal or the flange is loose.
  • ✓You need to flush twice or plunge more than once a week.
  • ✓A ring of water or staining shows on the ceiling below a second-floor bathroom.

Installing a new toilet

When we replace a toilet, we remove the old one, check the flange and the floor beneath it, and set the new toilet on a fresh seal. A damaged flange or a soft subfloor is easier to fix now than after the new toilet is in place.

You can buy the toilet yourself or let us help you choose. Look at the rough-in distance, which is the measure from the wall to the center of the drain, and at the bowl shape. Round bowls fit tight spaces, and elongated bowls are more comfortable. We confirm the fit before we order or install anything.

Saving water without giving up flush power

Modern toilets use far less water per flush than models from a few decades ago, and a good one clears the bowl in a single flush. Look for a model that carries the EPA WaterSense label, which means it meets a water efficiency and performance standard.

In a desert community where water is a cost and a concern, a leaking flapper is a quiet drain on the budget. A toilet that runs can waste a large amount of water over a month, and the fix is usually a small part. The leak detection page explains how to test the rest of the house if your bill stays high.

Toilets in Sahuarita homes

Many Sahuarita homes were built in the last few decades on concrete slabs, so toilet drains run through or beneath the slab. A base leak that soaks the floor may reach the subfloor but not an under-house space, which makes small leaks easy to miss until tile grout darkens or a baseboard swells.

Mineral buildup from local water can shorten the life of flappers and fill valves. If your parts wear out quickly, ask us about parts built to resist scale and whether it makes sense to test your water.

Water use standards for toilets

According to the EPA, a WaterSense labeled toilet uses 1.28 gallons per flush or less, and the federal standard maximum is 1.6 gallons per flush. Those numbers are printed on the product sheet, so you can check them before you buy.

If you are replacing an older toilet, the label is the quickest way to compare water use.

Toilet questions from Sahuarita homeowners

Can you fix a running toilet in one visit?

Usually yes. Most running toilets need a flapper, fill valve, or flush valve, and we carry common parts.

How do I stop an overflowing toilet?

Lift the tank lid and push the flapper down, or turn the shutoff valve behind the toilet clockwise. Then call us.

Why does my toilet rock?

The flange may be loose, the floor uneven, or the bolts loose. We reset it on a new seal and secure the flange.

Is a toilet that clogs often a sign of a bigger problem?

It can be. If other drains are slow too, the main line may be restricted. See drain cleaning.

Do you remove the old toilet?

Yes, hauling it away is part of the install.
